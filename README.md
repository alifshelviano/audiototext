## 📖 Project Overview

**LISN** is an intelligent meeting assistant designed to enhance productivity and collaboration in hybrid work environments. It leverages AI to provide real-time transcription, generate concise summaries, and offer deep insights into meeting dynamics.

### 🎯 Goal

The primary goal of LISN is to automate meeting documentation and analysis, allowing participants to focus on the discussion rather than on note-taking. By providing actionable summaries and a "Meeting Health Score," LISN helps teams improve their meeting quality over time.

### ✨ Main Features

-   **Real-Time Transcription:** Captures spoken words live during meetings.
-   **AI-Powered Summaries:** Generates structured summaries, including key decisions and action items.
-   **Meeting Health Score:** Analyzes metrics like engagement, productivity, and clarity to provide a comprehensive score.
-   **Interactive Q&A:** Allows users to ask questions about the meeting content and receive instant AI-generated answers.
-   **Secure Authentication:** Manages user accounts, profiles, and meeting history.
-   **Automated Email Reports:** Sends detailed meeting summaries directly to participants' inboxes.
-   **Easy Meeting Access:** Supports joining meetings via unique passkeys and QR codes.
